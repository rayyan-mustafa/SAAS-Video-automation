### FAQ

**Do I have to disclose that my videos use AI?**
On YouTube, realistic AI-generated content has to be disclosed, and Tillercast sets that disclosure automatically when it publishes. On other platforms, follow their labeling rules; we recommend labeling AI content.

**Will my videos get monetized?**
We can't promise that, and nobody honestly can. YouTube and other platforms judge originality and value, and they demonetize mass-produced, repetitive content. Tillercast gives you varied scripts, per-scene control and a consistent posting schedule. What you say and how good it is are still up to you.

**Is this "fully passive"?**
No. It takes the hours of editing off your plate, but the best results come from creators who pick good topics, review their videos and tweak scenes. Think of it as minutes per video instead of hours.

**What happens if a render fails?**
The credits are refunded to your account automatically. No support ticket needed.

**Who owns the videos?**
You do. You can use the videos you make commercially. Visuals and voices come from licensed providers and stock libraries under their terms. (Rayyan: confirm against your Terms before publishing this.)

**Can I edit a video before it goes out?**
Yes. Every scene can be rewritten, re-voiced or regenerated on its own, with undo history. You can also review before publishing instead of auto-posting.

**Which platforms do you publish to?**
YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels. Starter covers 1 channel, Creator covers YouTube Shorts + TikTok, Pro covers all 4.

**Can I cancel or change plans?**
Yes, any time from your dashboard. Founder pricing stays as long as your subscription stays active.

>
> How it works under the hood:
>
> - **Script:** an LLM (GPT-4o, Claude or Gemini) writes against one of 5 narrative frameworks, then the script is split into scenes, each with narration, a visual prompt and optional stat-card data.
> - **Voice:** per-scene TTS (Deepgram or ElevenLabs, or a cloned voice). Word timestamps drive the karaoke captions.
> - **Visuals:** each scene goes to a video model (Veo 3.1, Seedance, Kling) or gets a FLUX still with Ken Burns motion, with stock as the final fallback. Providers fail, rate-limit and time out a lot, so every scene has a fallback chain instead of failing the whole job.
> - **Render:** FFmpeg assembles the scenes, normalizes frame rates and resolutions, and burns in ASS subtitles positioned per platform safe zone. [Optional: the 59.9 fps subtitle bug story.]
> - **Credits:** every render reserves credits in a ledger, and the debit is only committed when the render succeeds. If a job fails, the reservation is reversed automatically, so users never pay for failures.
> - **Per-scene edits:** scenes are versioned, so re-voicing or regenerating one scene re-renders only what changed, and undo is just moving back to a previous version.
> - **Publishing:** platform APIs, with YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" flag set automatically.
>
> Hard parts I'd love to discuss: making fallbacks decent rather than jarring, keeping cost per video predictable with expensive video models, and caption timing after a scene is re-voiced.
>
> There's a paid product at [DOMAIN] (from $29/mo), but I'm mainly here for technical feedback. Happy to answer anything.
